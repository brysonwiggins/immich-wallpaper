# Privacy policy: Wallpapers with Immich

_Last updated: 2026-10-18_

**In short:** the app collects nothing. It talks only to the Immich server you set up.

## What the app stores, on your phone only
- **Your server address and settings:** sources, hidden photos, layout options.
- **Your Immich API key.** It is encrypted with a key held in Android's hardware-backed Keystore.
- **Prepared wallpapers and up to 10 offline photos,** downloaded from your server.
- **A diagnostics log.** It contains no API keys, server addresses or photo links. It leaves the phone only if you tap **Share** on it.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

## What the app sends, and where
- **Only to your own Immich server, using your API key:** requests for photos, albums and people, and photo downloads.
- **Nothing to the developer or to anyone else.** There are no ads, analytics, crash reporting or tracking.

## Google Play version
Tips are handled entirely by Google Play. The app never sees your payment details. Google's privacy policy
applies to those transactions.

## Permissions
- **Set wallpaper:** to change the wallpaper.
- **Internet and network state:** to reach your server and to wait for a connection before downloading.
- **Nearby devices (local network, Android 17 and later):** asked for only when your server is on your home network, so the app can reach it there.
- **Foreground service and notifications:** to notice the screen turning off. The notification shows that the app is running.
- **Run at startup:** to resume after a restart, if you had it on.
- **Ignore battery optimizations:** requested so the system doesn't stop the app overnight.
